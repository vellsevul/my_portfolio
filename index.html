01 / About

Code on one side, characters on the other.

I'm an Information Systems student at KBTU with two hackathons behind me. At hackathons I take the part of the project people actually see: the frontend, the animation, the look and feel.

I write in C++, Python and SQL, but I'm happiest where code meets visuals — a UI that moves well, a character that has a clear silhouette and a palette with a reason behind it.

I've drawn since long before I coded: original characters, sketches, manga-style comics. Lately I've been bringing drawings to life with AI image and video tools (Gemini, Veo), and I'm curious how far a single person can take that.

Filmmaking is the craft I want to try next.

Personal projectWeb appAlmaty

Zhyldam — moto-taxi app

A draft web app for a moto-taxi service in Almaty. The name comes from the Kazakh word for "fast". It has two sides: a passenger app and a rider app, with a Kazakh-flavoured identity and a Russian-language interface.

  • Passenger: pin-based pickup and destination on a map, address search, road routing, fixed-price or negotiated orders, live rider tracking, safety screen, trip sharing, history and receipts.
  • Rider: bike profile, nearby orders, accept or counter-offer, trip stages and earnings.
  • Light/dark theme, support chat, profile settings and cash / Kaspi / saved-card payment options (UI only, no real payments).
  • Stack: HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, Leaflet + OpenStreetMap, OSRM routing, Node.js server for the live connection.
